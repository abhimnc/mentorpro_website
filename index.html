Questions

Can't find what you're looking for? Write to info@mentorpro.in.

How much does a session cost?

Each mentor sets their own rate, shown per minute on their profile. You'll see the price before you book.

How do I pay?

Payments are processed in the app by Cashfree Payments. You can pay with the usual Indian payment methods. ProMentors never receives your full card or bank details.

Are calls recorded?

No. Audio and video are streamed live for the call and are not recorded or stored by us.

Which devices are supported?

ProMentors is available for Android on Google Play.

How do I delete my account?

Email info@mentorpro.in from the email address on your account and ask for it to be deleted. We process verified requests within 30 days. See our privacy policy for details.
