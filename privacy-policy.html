Privacy Policy

ProMentors · Last updated 25 July 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how ProMentors (“we”, “us”, the “App”) collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our mobile application. By using ProMentors, you agree to the practices described here.

1. Information We Collect

CategoryDataWhy
Account informationName, email address, phone number, passwordTo create and secure your account and let mentors and mentees identify one another.
Audio & videoLive camera and microphone streams during callsTo provide real-time video consultations between mentors and mentees. These streams are transmitted for the live call and are not recorded or stored by us.

2. Device Permissions

The App requests the following permissions on your device:

  • Camera and Microphone — for live video consultations. Access is used only during an active call.
  • Notifications — to alert you about bookings, calls, and messages.
  • Bluetooth — to route call audio to connected devices such as headphones.
  • Network access — to connect to our services.

You can revoke camera, microphone, notification, and Bluetooth permissions at any time in your device settings.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To operate and provide the App's features (accounts, mentor discovery, bookings, calls, messaging).
  • To process payments for consultations.
  • To send transactional notifications you have enabled.
  • To maintain security and prevent fraud or abuse.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use it for third-party advertising.

4. Third-Party Services

We rely on the following service providers, each of which processes some data on our behalf under their own privacy policies:

  • Agora — real-time audio/video calling. Agora Privacy Policy
  • Google Firebase — push notifications and core services. Firebase Privacy
  • Cashfree Payments — payment processing. Cashfree Privacy Policy

5. Data Sharing

We share information only: (a) with the service providers listed above to operate the App; (b) between mentors and mentees to the extent needed to arrange and conduct a consultation (for example, your display name); and (c) when required by law or to protect our rights and the safety of users.

6. Data Retention

We keep your account information for as long as your account is active. Live audio and video streams are not retained after a call ends. When you delete your account, we delete or anonymize your personal data except where we are required to retain it (for example, payment records for tax or legal purposes).

7. Account & Data Deletion

You may request deletion of your account and associated personal data at any time by emailing us at the address below. We will process verified requests within 30 days.

8. Data Security

We use industry-standard measures, including encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS), to protect your data in transit. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work to protect your information against unauthorized access.

9. Children's Privacy

ProMentors is intended for users who are able to form a binding agreement, or who use the App under the supervision and consent of a parent or guardian. If you believe a child has provided us personal data without appropriate consent, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data, or to object to certain processing. To exercise these rights, contact us using the details below.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the “Last updated” date at the top of this page.
